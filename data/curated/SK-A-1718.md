## timeline
A whole society on one frozen river, seen from high up so that nobody outranks anybody
else: skaters, kolf players, people out walking, a horse-drawn sleigh at the right, a
church at the left. Avercamp painted it around 1608, after the bitter winter of 1607–08,
and was the first Dutch painter to make snow his speciality.

## closer

## detail
The picture owes a great deal to Pieter Bruegel the Elder's Winter Landscape with a Bird
Trap of 1565, and the bird trap itself reappears here at the left, among the farm
implements. Avercamp used the motif in other paintings too.

Nothing has been prettified. A dog is at a carcass in the lower left corner, and in the
foreground a group of men are working to free a boat frozen into the ice. The museum
bought the panel in 1897 with the support of the Vereniging Rembrandt.
