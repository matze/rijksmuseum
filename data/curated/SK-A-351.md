## timeline
Two-thirds sky. Ruisdael stood on the dunes north-west of Haarlem, put the horizon a
third of the way up and gave the rest to cloud. Down in the strip of land, lengths of
linen lie out on the grass to bleach.

## closer
Find the shadow of a cloud on the fields. That is what the sky is for.

## detail
Haarlem's linen industry ran on the clean water of the dunes, and bleaching was done by
laying the cloth out in the sun for weeks. The white rectangles in the middle distance
are that: an industrial process, painted as pattern.

On the skyline is the town, and the tall roof standing over it is St Bavo's — the same
church Saenredam painted from the inside, a few bays along this gallery. Ruisdael painted
this view from the dunes more than once.
