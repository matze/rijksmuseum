## timeline
Saint Anthony prays in the left corner while, across the water, temptation waits: a naked
woman standing in a house that is also an old woman's head.

## closer
The house on the right is the head of an old woman, a bawd, with a dovecote perched on
top. In the 16th century a dovecote meant a brothel.

## detail
Anthony leans on a rock where an apple and a wooden staff lie. On his cloak is the Tau
cross of the Antonine monks, probably a reference to the saint's crutch-shaped staff.

A river with a small boat steered by a woman separates the praying saint from the scene
of temptation. In the background a monastery burns, and monsters fly through the air.

## look
1. Anthony praying, leaning on the rock with the apple and staff.
2. The house shaped like an old woman's head, with the dovecote on top.
3. The naked woman inside the house.

## kids
The house is also a face. Can you find the eyes and the nose?
