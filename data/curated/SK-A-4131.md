## timeline
Mary and Joseph kneel over a stone trough where the infant lies, while a band of angels
sings above them.

## closer
This panel adds the singing angels above the crib, which is what turns the scene from
tall to wide.

## detail
The museum's note says the picture is based on a Nativity with half-length figures known
in versions in Brussels and Cologne. The Amsterdam panel gained a group of music-making
angels above the crib, which gives it its horizontal shape. Those angels also appear in
fifteenth-century Nativities by Hugo van der Goes.

The ox and the donkey stand by the trough. A shepherd peeps from behind a curtain. Two
men warm themselves at a fire on the left, and in the distance on the right is the
annunciation to the shepherds.

## look
1. The group of angels making music above the crib.
2. The ox and the donkey beside the trough.
3. The shepherd watching from behind the curtain.
