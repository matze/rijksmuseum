## timeline
Pilate presents the thorn-crowned Christ to a jeering crowd, a scene Bosch painted early
in his career and his followers copied for decades.

## closer
The two oak planks have cracked at top and bottom, and the varnish has discoloured. The
scene reads dimmer now than it was painted.

## detail
The moment is John 19: Pilate shows Christ, crowned with thorns, to the people, saying
"Behold the man". The painting is a fairly faithful copy of the Ecce Homo in Frankfurt,
painted around 1480-1490 and commonly accepted as an early work by Bosch.

The original carried the words in gold lettering: "Ecce Homo", "Crucifige eum" (Crucify
him), and an appeal to the Redeemer on the corner of the rostrum. This copy leaves those
inscriptions out.

## look
1. Christ, crowned with thorns, at the centre.
2. Pilate presenting him.
3. The jeering faces of the crowd.
