## detail
This is a wall, not a picture. It is a fragment of a painted room decoration, made in
1727 for Jan Baptist de Surmont, lord of Vlooswijk, for his house at Loenen aan de Vecht,
and it is two and a half metres tall. Its companion piece hangs beside it.

De Wit spent his career painting rooms — ceilings, overdoors, wall pieces, several more of
them elsewhere on this floor — in a light, high-keyed manner made for living in rather
than for looking at across a gallery. He signed and dated
this on the rock in the middle, below the eagle. Around the edges of the scene the props
of the disguise are laid out: Diana's hunting gear at the lower right, her dogs beside it,
a quiver hanging in the tree, and putti at the left with more of the same.
