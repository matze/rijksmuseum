## detail
Saenredam painted almost nothing but architecture. He measured and drew buildings on the
spot in enormous detail, then worked the drawings up into panels years later; this one
carries the date 1636, painted on the small organ in the chapel at the right.

The eye goes up to the old organ, and on its shutter is a Resurrection of Christ. By 1636
the church had been Protestant for decades and the Catholic furnishing had long been
stripped out — the painted shutter is one of the pieces that survived, hanging in a
building that no longer had a use for it.
