## detail
Alma-Tadema was Frisian, moved to England, was knighted there, and became one of the most
popular painters of the nineteenth century on the strength of pictures like this: the
ancient world reconstructed detail by detail, and then a single ordinary emotion put in
the middle of it.

He painted this in 1872, on panel, and signed it on the sarcophagus. The Egyptian
architecture, the painted columns, the inlaid floor and the instruments are all worked up
from what was known at the time — which is why the picture is also a document of what
1872 believed Egypt had looked like.
