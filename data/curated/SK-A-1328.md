## timeline
Mounted artillery coming down a dune at full speed, straight at you. Breitner painted the
uniforms — black and red busbies, gold braid — as a set of colour accents and then let
the sand thrown up by the front horses swallow everyone behind.

## closer
The back half of the squadron is not painted. It is a dust cloud with three colours left
in it, and it works.

## detail
The Yellow Riders were an elite mounted artillery corps. What interests Breitner is not
the regiment, though: it is the movement. The repetition of black, yellow and red down
the line is what makes the charge read as speed rather than as a row of horsemen.

The picture is upright, which is unusual for a cavalry subject — a charge is a horizontal
event — and the format is what pushes the horses out of the canvas towards the viewer
instead of across it.
