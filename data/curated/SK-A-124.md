## timeline
Two kings kneel before the Christ child while a third stands waiting, in a small panel
copied from a famous Bosch altarpiece now in Madrid.

## closer
This is the centre panel of a triptych, reduced and reworked. The wings and the arched top
are gone.

## detail
The museum's own note calls it a late and greatly reduced copy of the centre panel of
Bosch's Adoration of the Magi triptych in Madrid, painted around 1510. Bosch's followers
copied it again and again. Like the versions in Berlin and Philadelphia, this one keeps
only the central scene, with no wings and no arched top with a landscape.

The record's description adds what is in the picture. Mary sits before the stable with the
child. Two kings kneel and a third stands. Shepherds have gathered around. A precious
helmet lies in the foreground, and a city rises in the distance.

## look
1. The precious helmet in the foreground, set down as a king came to kneel.
2. The third king, still standing, waiting his turn.
3. The city in the distance.
