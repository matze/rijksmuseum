## timeline
Münster town hall, 15 May 1648: hands go up around a green table, an oath is sworn, and
eighty years of war with Spain end. Ter Borch was in the room. He painted it on a sheet
of copper, small enough that every head in it is smaller than a thumbnail.

## detail
The Peace of Münster ended the revolt against Spain and recognised the Dutch Republic.
Ter Borch painted the ratification itself: the delegates of both sides around one table,
named one by one in the museum's record, with the treaty documents lying in their boxes
on the green cloth in front of them.

He signed it on the painted wall at the upper left, "made at Münster, 1648". He also put
himself in: the figure at the extreme left edge, looking out of the picture, is the
painter. The support is copper, which is why the detail holds at this scale.
