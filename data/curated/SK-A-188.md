## timeline
Roses, poppies, peonies, narcissi, irises, cornflowers, fritillaries, hyacinths, a crown
imperial and tulips, in one earthenware vase. None of them flower at the same time of
year, so no such bouquet was ever put in a vase: it was assembled on the panel.
region: 0.2950 0.0300 0.3100 0.1900

## closer
Look for the insects. There are more than you expect, and each one is painted at the size
it really is.

## detail
Flower painting was long established by the time Van Huysum took it up, and what he
changed was the background: instead of the customary dark ground he put in a pale park
— a statue of Flora at the left, some loosely brushed foliage, a pond — so the flowers
seem to stand out of doors on a balustrade rather than in a closed room.
region: "a statue of Flora at the left" 0.0150 0.1000 0.1500 0.3200

The panel is signed and dated 1723 at the lower left. Under the flowers the vase is
modelled with playing putti, and at the right corner of the ledge there is a bird's nest
with eggs in it, painted as carefully as anything in the bouquet.
