## timeline
Not a portrait. This is a tronie — a character head, painted to be a type rather than a
person — and in the seventeenth century pictures like this were called Turkish tronies.
The man wears a turban with a gold chain and a single large pearl.

## closer
The lighting is the subject. The turban and the right side of his face are brilliantly
lit; the left side is simply given away to shadow.

## detail
Tronies sold. They were popular, widely copied and imitated, and they let a painter show
off costume, texture and light without a client telling him what the sitter should look
like. Nobody knows who sat for this one, if anybody did.

The turban is not documentary either. It is studio property standing in for a whole
imagined East, of the kind Amsterdam traded with and did not otherwise look at closely.
