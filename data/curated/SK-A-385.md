## timeline
The morning of the sixth of December, and the presents have been handed out unevenly.
The small girl in the middle has a bucket of sweets and a doll and will not let either
go. Her brother, on the left, has been crying since he looked in his shoe.

## closer
Every person in this room is reacting to the same event, and no two of them the same
way. That is the whole trick of Jan Steen.

## detail
Sinterklaas has been kept in more or less this form for centuries: good children get
presents from the saint, bad ones get a birch rod in their shoe. The boy on the left got
the rod. His grandmother is beckoning him from behind the bed curtain — there is
something for him after all, hidden there — and the laughing boy beside their mother, with
a kolf stick in his hand, is enjoying the situation far too much.
region: "the laughing boy beside their mother" 0.5200 0.4750 0.1350 0.2400

Steen was a storyteller before he was anything else, and he has got every part of the
festival into one room: the bucket of treats, the doll of John the Baptist in the
girl's arms, the basket of bread and spiced biscuit on the floor, the children singing
by the chimney where the saint is supposed to come down.
