## closer
The picture is built on one straight line: doorway, passage, front door, street. Stand
where you can look right down it.

## detail
The cupboard is the point. In a household of this class the linen press was the store of
the family's most valuable movable goods, and the mistress of the house carried its key
and handed out the sheets. Painting a woman at an open linen cupboard is painting a woman
in charge of something.
region: "the mistress of the house carried its key" 0.1550 0.4950 0.0550 0.0500

Everything else is arranged around that transaction: the child at her game, the light from
the street, the small sculpture on the doorframe. Nothing dramatic happens, and the whole
composition is engineered so that you notice.
