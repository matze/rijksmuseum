## timeline
Van Gogh saved up, came to Amsterdam to see the newly opened Rijksmuseum, stood in front
of Hals and Rembrandt, walked out shaken by how loosely they had painted — and then set
up on the Singel and painted this in a hurry, on a panel he had brought in his box.

## closer
This is 1885. Nothing about it looks like the Van Gogh you know, except the speed.

## detail
This is a year before he moved to Paris and met Impressionism, and it is still the dark
palette of his Dutch years. What he had just seen were the seventeenth-century masters in
this building, and what struck him was the brushwork rather than the subjects.

The panel is small enough to carry in one hand. The dome across the water belongs to the
Lutheran church on the Singel; the light is grey, the water is grey, and the whole thing
is put down in strokes that were plainly not gone back over.
