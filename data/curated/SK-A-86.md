## detail
Dou trained with Rembrandt in Leiden from the age of fourteen and then went the other
way entirely, into the small, polished manner of the Leiden fijnschilders. He is said to
have spent five days painting a hand, and to have made his own brushes because none he
could buy were fine enough. The stone niche with a sill is the device he is known for,
and the whole point of it is to let things lean out at the viewer.

The book on the ledge overhangs the stone and reaches into the room; the curtain hangs
from rings on a rod, so that at first glance it is the curtain over the picture rather
than a part of it. The signature is on the sheet of paper below the book.
