## timeline
Cockatoos from Australia, a grey parrot from Africa, a lory from Indonesia, squirrel
monkeys from Central America — all arranged on a garden balustrade around a stone vase.
The painting hung over the door of William III's private apartment at Het Loo.

## closer
Nothing in this picture could have met anything else in it. That is the point of a
menagerie, and the point of an empire.

## detail
A royal menagerie was a collection in the same sense as a cabinet of curiosities: living
proof of how far the ships went. Hondecoeter specialised in painting them, and he paints
each bird as an individual — plumage, posture, eye — rather than as a decorative motif,
which is why the picture still works as a picture and not only as an inventory.

The signature is under the vase. The balustrade, the oriental carpet thrown over it and
the dark oak at the right are all stage furniture; the animals are the subject, and there
are more of them than a first look finds.
