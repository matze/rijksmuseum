## detail
Jan Steen dated it 1655 and set it precisely: the tower of the Oude Kerk stands behind,
and the bridge over the Oude Delft carries the arms of the town. For most of its life the
sitters were unknown and the picture went under the name 'The Burgomaster of Delft and his
Daughter' — a title it has kept in speech although the man was never the burgomaster.
Research on the cityscape and the sitters in 2006 identified them as Adolf Croeser and his
daughter Catharina.
region: "the tower of the Oude Kerk stands behind" 0.7450 0.2200 0.0800 0.2300

It hung at Penrhyn Castle in Wales for over a century. The Rijksmuseum bought it in July
2004 for 11.9 million euros, the most it has ever paid for a painting, after the British
government held back the export licence to give British institutions a chance to match the
offer.
