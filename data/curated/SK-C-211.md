## closer
Two thirds of the canvas is weather. Watch how much of the land is in shadow, and how
little of the water the light actually reaches.

## detail
Seymour Slive, who catalogued Ruisdael's paintings, noted that the detail holds up to
specialist reading: the set of the sails and the movement of the water are both correct.
The canvas is signed 'Ruisdael' at the lower right but carries no date — Ruisdael dated
almost nothing after 1653 — so the museum gives it as about 1668 to 1670.

The mill is gone; its foundations can still be seen. Another mill a few hundred metres
away is regularly mistaken for it, a confusion that goes back to a description published
in 1911. The painting itself belongs to the City of Amsterdam, from the Van der Hoop
bequest, and has been on loan to the Rijksmuseum since 1885.
