## timeline
An unnamed man from Haarlem, painted by Jan van Scorel in 1529 on an arched panel against
flat orange-red. No coat of arms, no motto, no attributes. Just a face, and a fur-lined
coat he is holding shut.
region: 0.2600 0.2100 0.4300 0.3000

## closer
The background is one colour, laid on flat. Everything the picture does, it does with the
head.

## detail
Van Scorel spent 1518 to 1524 in Italy, and his stay happened to coincide with the reign
of the only Dutch pope, Adrian VI, who made him court painter and put him in charge of
the papal antiquities. He came back one of the first northern painters to have absorbed
the Italian manner at first hand, and the solidity of this head is what he brought home.

The arched top is not a frame: it is the shape of the panel, cut that way. Against it the
red ground pushes the head forward, which is the whole device — no interior, no landscape,
nothing behind him at all.
