## timeline
Two riders have stopped at the edge of a river and one horse is drinking. Behind them a
hillside goes gold in low sun, cattle stand about, and a town shows its towers in the
far distance. The canvas is over two metres wide and almost all of it is light.

## detail
Cuyp was born and died in Dordrecht, was taught by his father and is known above all for
large river views in a golden early-morning or late-afternoon light. That light is what
he is doing here: coming in low from one side, it turns every upright thing into a
silhouette with a gilded edge. The hill it falls on is not a Dutch hill.

He signed it at the bottom centre. It was bought for the nation with support from the
Vereniging Rembrandt, and hangs in the Gallery of Honour among the pictures the museum
puts at the front of the queue.
