## timeline
Sixty-two years old, in black silk and fur, with her family's arms on the wall behind
her and a silver-mounted church book on her lap. Hals wrote her age and the year into
the wall — ÆTAT SVA 62, ANº 1639 — so there is no arguing with either.

## closer
The face is painted at speed and the ruff is painted at leisure. Both are Hals, and the
difference is the point.

## detail
Maritge Vooght came from Haarlem brewers and married Pieter Olycan at eighteen. The
marriage let him take high office in the town and made him co-heir to her family's
brewery; the couple then founded a second brewery of their own, registered — as the
period required — in his name alone. Their daughter Geertruyt ran the business on.

The portrait is a knee-length seated one, and probably the companion to a portrait of
her husband now in Sarasota. The wealth is stated by the clothing and by the book, which
has silver corner mounts and a silver clasp and is the most expensive object in the
picture.
