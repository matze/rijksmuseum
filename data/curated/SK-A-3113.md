## timeline
By torchlight, a crowd of half-length figures closes in on Christ: the soldier, the
traitor Judas, and Peter lunging with his dagger.

## closer
Every figure is cut off at the waist, so the faces crowd together and the struggle fills
the whole frame.

## detail
On the right, Saint Peter in a red robe raises his dagger over Malchus, the servant of
the high priest. Malchus bites Peter's arm and shoves a lantern into his face. Next to
Christ's calm face is the head of Judas, and two more men close in.

The museum notes the group is very like the left wing of the Triptych with Christ Crowned
with Thorns in Valencia. It dates this panel to around 1530-1550 and attributes it to the
manner of Bosch.

## look
1. Christ's face in the middle of the crowd.
2. Peter and Malchus struggling on the right.
3. The torch and lantern, the only light in the scene.
