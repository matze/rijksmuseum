## timeline

## detail
Showing the processions in miniature is the point of the picture: the kings were
understood to have come from Europe, Asia and Africa, so the whole known world is arriving
at one shed in the same instant.

An x-ray of the underdrawing found a change of mind. In Melchior's European train, an
animal first drawn as a horse became a dromedary — surprising, because in Adoration
paintings the camels usually belong to Balthasar, who was said to have come from Ethiopia.
The panel's history can be traced no further back than 1904, when the museum bought it.
