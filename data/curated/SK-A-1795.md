## timeline
Saint Anthony kneels in prayer while a seductive woman and a crowd of demons press in
around him, a copy of the central panel of Bosch's famous Lisbon altarpiece.

## closer
This panel was once the middle section of a triptych. It repeats the central group of
figures from Bosch's Temptation of St Anthony in Lisbon.

## detail
Bosch painted the Lisbon triptych around 1500-1510, and it was copied many times. The
best copy, a signed one now in Brussels, is the earliest, from around 1520-1530. This
one belongs to that long line of copies.

A burning city glows in the background. A city on fire is the traditional sign of
Anthony's protection against ergotism, the burning sickness his monks treated, also
called Saint Anthony's fire.

## look

2. The seductive woman among the demons.
3. The burning city in the background.
