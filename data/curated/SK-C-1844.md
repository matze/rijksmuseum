## timeline
Two summers after the grey Amsterdam panel hanging beside it, Van Gogh is in Paris and
everything has changed: short strokes, bare canvas showing through, and a bank of the
Seine near Asnières painted in blue, pink and green.

## closer
Stand where you can see this and the Singel panel at once. Two years, one painter, no

## detail
He moved to Paris in 1886 to learn what the French Impressionists were doing, and this is
that lesson being practised: bright colour, broken touch, and a composition that crops
the subject as a photograph would rather than composing it as a landscape should.

Asnières, north-east of the city along the river, is where he spent the summer of 1887
painting outdoors. The subject is nothing — a slope of sand, some grass, a few slim
trunks — and the picture is all in the marks.
