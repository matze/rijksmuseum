## timeline
Magelang, central Java, 28 March 1830. Prince Diponegoro has come to negotiate under a
promise of safe conduct and has been arrested instead. This painting was commissioned by
the man who broke that promise, and it is not a record of what happened.
region: 0.4900 0.3300 0.1400 0.2700

## closer
Compare the two halves. Everything Dutch is upright and lit; everything Javanese is on
the ground.

## detail
Diponegoro led the Java War of 1825 to 1830 against Dutch rule. Lieutenant General
Hendrik Merkus de Kock had him seized at the peace talks and later commissioned Nicolaas
Pieneman to paint the scene. What Pieneman produced shows no treachery: it shows a prince
submitting. The Dutch officers stand on the veranda of the Resident's house, the tricolour
is at full mast, and Diponegoro is being handed over to Major De Stuers for the carriage
waiting on the left.
region: "the tricolour is at full mast" 0.4450 0.0250 0.1200 0.1000

In the left foreground his followers sit on the ground with their weapons laid down in
front of them. The museum's own note says plainly what the picture is doing and who paid
for it, which is the only reason a painting like this can still be hung.
