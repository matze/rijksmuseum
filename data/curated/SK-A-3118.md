## timeline
Thomas Gresham was the English crown's financial agent in the Low Countries, and founded
both the London exchange and Gresham College. Here he sits in black silk with his gloves
in one hand, painted around 1560 by Anthonis Mor.
region: 0.2400 0.1300 0.2600 0.2300

## closer
The face is all edges and the sleeves are all shine. Notice which one Mor spent longer on.

## detail
Mor's manner is cool, exact and without flattery. He gives Gresham a plain brown ground,
a red chair and no attributes at all except the gloves — no ledger, no coin, nothing at
all to say what the sitter did for a living.

The portrait has a companion: Gresham's wife, Anne Fernely, painted to match and hanging
beside him. They are meant to be read as a pair, each turned slightly towards the other.
