## timeline
An oak cupboard from about 1525, standing on an open frame, with its whole front cut
through into stone-style tracery. It belonged to a militia guild in Alkmaar, and it is
Gothic architecture built at the size of furniture.
region: 0.1400 0.2100 0.7000 0.2900

## closer
The front is not carved in relief. It is pierced right through, so the shadow behind it
is part of the design.

## detail
The four panels across the front carry different devices worked into the tracery: a
fleur-de-lis, a firesteel with crossed arrows under an imperial crown, and a firesteel
with a flame under a royal crown. The upright between the two drawers ends in an angel
holding a shield. It is heraldry, and it names the company's allegiances without a word.

The sides and back have linenfold panelling; the feet are blocks carved as lions. The
whole thing is a hundred and forty-seven centimetres high, which is the size of a piece
made to stand in a guild hall and be looked at rather than to hold much.
