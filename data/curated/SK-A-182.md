## timeline
A maidservant hands a lidded jug down to a small child, on a tiled floor, between two
open doorways. That is the entire event, and De Hooch has built a whole architecture
around it.
region: 0.3900 0.4550 0.1200 0.1450

## closer
Two doors, two different lights. Stand where you can see both at once and the room
suddenly has a plan.

## detail
De Hooch was known for the doorkijkje — the view from one room through into another —
and this picture has two of them. On the left a dark opening leads down to the cellar;
on the right a passage runs to the front room, where the daylight coming through the
window is painted as pure white.
region: "the daylight coming through the window is painted as pure white" 0.6350 0.2250 0.2700 0.5200

The child is in a dress with long bands hanging from the shoulders. Every small child
wore them: they are walking reins, held by an adult behind while the child learned to
stay upright. They are the sort of ordinary thing this painter noticed and almost nobody
else bothered to.
region: "they are walking reins" 0.4350 0.5450 0.1700 0.1400
