## timeline
A pink track climbing a Mediterranean headland at midday, painted by Monet in 1884. The
museum was given it in 1900, when almost nobody in the Netherlands had heard of him, and
it is the only Monet the museum currently has on view.

## closer
Stand back four paces. The scrub in the foreground stops being dabs and becomes a hillside.

## detail
What is now the main road between Nice and Monaco was a narrow mountain track when Monet
walked it. The sun is high — you can tell from the walker's shadow, which is almost
directly under him — and the whole picture is built out of that: red road, blue rock,
green scrub, everything vibrating against everything else.
region: "the walker's shadow, which is almost directly under him" 0.5600 0.5750 0.0600 0.0900

The gift came from Baroness van Lynden-van Pallandt in 1900. French Impressionism was
still an oddity here; Dutch collectors of the period were buying the Hague School, which
is what hangs on either side of it in this room.
