## timeline
Van Gogh moved to Paris in 1886 after his brother Theo wrote to him about the new,
colourful French painting. He tried it out on himself, in one self-portrait after another,
mostly because it saved paying a model. Here he is a fashionably dressed Parisian, built
out of rhythmic strokes in striking colour.

## closer
The background is not a colour but hundreds of separate strokes. Step back three paces and
they mix in your eye instead of on the palette.

## detail
It is painted on cardboard rather than canvas — cheap, absorbent, and unforgiving of
second thoughts. He was two years out from the brown peasant pictures of Nuenen.

Paris is where the palette changed, and a small board like this is where the change is
easiest to watch happening. Nothing is blended. Colours are set beside each other and left
to do their work at viewing distance.
