## timeline
Three metres wide and nearly three high, in coromandel wood inlaid with purpleheart,
padauk and boxwood: a sideboard from about 1902 with heraldic beasts on every door,
carved figures across the crest and porcelain standing in its glazed cabinets.
region: 0.0800 0.6500 0.8000 0.2600

## closer
Every door carries a coat of arms and no two are the same. Work along the row.

## detail
The lower stage has five bays, the three middle ones with paired doors under a drawer, the
outer two set on the diagonal to turn the corner. Each door panel is carved with a crowned
shield between two facing lions in foliage, and each shield is different; the corner
panels carry flower scrollwork instead. The drawers are inlaid with ivory and boxwood, and
every lock plate and hinge is a brass mask or a beast.
region: "each shield is different" 0.1000 0.7000 0.7600 0.2000

Above that a set-back stage holds four glazed cabinets between carved half-columns and
figures, and above that again the crest: three pierced rails, the uppermost stepped back,
carried on nine uprights of which seven end in carved figures and two in coats of arms.
The bottoms of those uprights are lions' heads, or bulls' heads, or two figures twisted
together.
