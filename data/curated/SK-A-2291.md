## timeline
The Trekvliet canal outside The Hague on a bright day in 1868, with barges under sail, a
farm on the left and a woman and children on the path. Weissenbruch changed nothing: the
tower on the left horizon and the mill on the right were both really there.

## closer
Two-thirds sky again, and this time the clouds have edges. Follow one from left to right
and watch it come apart.

## detail
The Trekvliet linked The Hague to Rijswijk and Voorburg. In the distance at the left is
the tower of De Binckhorst castle; on the right is the windmill called the Laakmolen. The
museum's own note makes the point that this is topography rather than invention — the
Romantics before him would have moved the mill.
region: "on the right is the windmill called the Laakmolen" 0.6700 0.5700 0.0800 0.1200

The painting is early Weissenbruch and still Romantic in its brightness, on a panel half a
metre wide. He signed and dated it at the lower right.
