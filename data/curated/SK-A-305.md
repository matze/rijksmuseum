## timeline
The back of Amsterdam's town hall from the Nieuwezijds Voorburgwal, about 1785, with the
Nieuwe Kerk beside it and its tower still unfinished — scaffolded, stopped, and never
going any higher.

## closer
Ouwater paints the city the way an estate agent would: every window counted. Then he
fills the street with people who are not looking at any of it.

## detail
The Nieuwe Kerk's tower was begun and then abandoned, long before Ouwater set up here, and
he paints it as it stood: a stump with scaffolding on it. Beside it the town hall turns its
plain back elevation to us rather than the ceremonial front on the Dam. That is the whole
point of the view — this is the working side of both buildings.

The street does the rest. There are walkers, elegant women and a gentleman, dogs, horses
and carts, and a man dredging the canal. The picture has a companion in the museum, and
Ouwater signed this one at the lower left.
