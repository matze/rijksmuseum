## timeline
A wide hilly landscape under a great rock, and a battle running through it. Indigenous
people with bows, sticks and flails are defending themselves against Spanish troops with
lances, halberds, firearms and cannon. Mostaert painted it around 1535, in Haarlem,
without ever having crossed the Atlantic.

## closer
Look at the weapons on each side. The picture states the outcome in the equipment before
it states anything else.

## detail
The museum calls it the first painting to show Spanish soldiers subduing the people who
lived there. Mostaert worked in Haarlem all his life and knew the subject only from
report, so the setting is an ordinary European landscape with exotica added to it: a
monkey, a porcupine, parrots.

It is his most famous painting and it took a coalition to buy it: the lottery, the
Vereniging Rembrandt, three national funds, and the family of the dealer Jacques
Goudstikker, in his memory.
