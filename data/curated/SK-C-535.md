## detail
The Dutch title — het gebed zonder end, the prayer without end — is the name of the joke:
she prays, the meal goes. Maes painted it around 1656, a few years after leaving
Rembrandt's studio, and the debt shows in the light, which comes from one side and leaves
most of the room in brown darkness.

In the niche behind her is a small still life doing quiet work: a bunch of keys, a funnel
and an hourglass. The hourglass is time running out, and it is hanging directly above an
old woman at her last meal of the day.
