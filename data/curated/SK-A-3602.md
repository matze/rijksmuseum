## timeline
Three riders coming down off the dune towards the sea, seen from behind, with the bathing
cabins already wheeled out on the sand. The Hague School usually painted the fishermen at
Scheveningen; this is the other Scheveningen, the one with money.

## closer
Almost the whole painting is two colours: sand and sky. Find the third one.

## detail
Mauve worked the coast north of The Hague, and the ordinary subject there was hard labour
— boats hauled up, nets, weather. This is the same beach at the sociable end of the
morning, and it is painted in a blond and blue scheme that does the salt air more
convincingly than any amount of detail would.

Everyone is seen from behind, riding away, which is the decision the picture turns on: no
faces, no story, just three people and a fourth in the distance going down to the water.
