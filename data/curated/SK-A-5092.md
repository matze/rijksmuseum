## timeline

## detail
An ensign carried the colours of a civic guard company and was expected to die rather
than lose them. Rembrandt gives him a column at his back — a standard emblem of
steadfastness — a gorget at his throat, a sword, and sixteenth-century dress with
slashed sleeves that was already costume by 1636. The light comes hard from the upper
left and lands on the banner and on his right cuff.

The Dutch State bought the painting in 2021, with the Vereniging Rembrandt and its
members contributing, after it had spent generations in the Rothschild collection in
Paris. It had been described as a self-portrait as early as 1836; the face under the
moustache is the one from all the other self-portraits.
