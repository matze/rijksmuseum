## closer
The shawl is the loudest thing Maes ever painted. Then look at the hand holding the
blossom, which is the quietest.

## detail
Maes began as a Rembrandt pupil painting dark interiors and old women — the praying old
woman a few rooms from here is his — went home to Dordrecht for twenty years, and came
back to Amsterdam to become the leading portrait painter of the city's last decades of
the century. This is that late manner: a knee-length pose against a park, blue dress,
orange wrap, painted between 1677 and 1685.

It is one of a pair. Petronella Dunois married Pieter Groenendijk in 1677, and Maes
painted him too; the two portraits belong together.
