## timeline
Seventeen centimetres of gilt bronze, made in China and dated by its own inscription to
the year 512. A bodhisattva stands on a lotus pedestal against a leaf-shaped halo of
carved flame, on a little four-legged stand.
region: 0.3400 0.3200 0.2600 0.4000

## closer
It is the size of your hand and it is fifteen hundred years old. Both of those facts are
worth a moment.

## detail
Guanyin is a bodhisattva — in Buddhist terms, one who has reached enlightenment. The
sides of the pedestal carry the inscriptions that give the date, which is why this small
object matters out of all proportion to its size: most early Chinese bronzes of this kind
have to be dated by style, and this one dates itself.

The drapery falls in tight parallel folds, a way of carving that came into China with
Buddhist figures from India. Everything behind the figure — the mandorla, the flames
worked into it in fine repeated curls — is one cast piece with the rest.
